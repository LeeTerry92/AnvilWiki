Emberfang, the Ash Warden, is the first major boss most players encounter in Anvil Quest, guarding the exit of the Frostspire Approach at the end of the tutorial arc. This guide covers every attack pattern with its damage numbers, the safest windows to deal damage, the loadouts that make this fight trivial, and the mistakes that cause nine out of ten wipes. If you can beat Emberfang comfortably, you are ready for the mid-game; if you can't, he is a precise diagnostic of what to fix.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick tip">Any ice-imbued weapon shortens this fight by roughly a third. Frostpike is craftable two quests before the arena gate — see the [forging guide](/guides/forging-guide/) for the exact recipe chain.</Callout>

## Before You Pull

Thirty seconds of preparation removes most of the run-to-run randomness:

| Preparation | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Level 8+ (9 is comfortable) | Emberfang hits for 180 per bolt; level 7 HP pools give no margin |
| Ice weapon (Frostpike ideal) | Fire-weak boss — ice deals double, fire deals a quarter |
| 3–5 Health Potions | Each Potion covers one mistake; five covers a learning attempt |
| 1 Greater Frost Potion | Cancels the Phase 3 scorch entirely (see below) |
| Clear the arena trash first | The two Ash Hounds respawn if you die and don't clear them |

The potions cost 25–60 gold each at Mara's shop. If you're short, redeem the latest codes from our [codes page](/codes/) — FORGE-2026 alone covers the full shopping list.

Emberfang is a gatekeeper boss. The good news: he has a strict, learnable pattern — three phases, each with exactly one mechanic, and each mechanic has exactly one correct answer. Nothing in this fight is random beyond which side the first bolt volley starts from.

## Phase 1: Fire Bolt Volley (100% – 66% HP)

Emberfang opens the fight by firing three fire bolts in a spread pattern every 3 seconds. The bolts travel at medium speed and deal 180 damage on a direct hit — survivable, but three hits in a row will end a level 8 attempt.

**Counter**: Strafe right. All three bolts fan out left-to-right, so constant rightward movement dodges every volley without burning your dash.

The rhythm is: three strides right while he winds up, one or two hits of your own between volleys, repeat. A full Phase 1 at level 8 takes roughly 90 seconds with the Frostpike, 150 without.

## Phase 2: Burning Ground (66% – 33% HP)

At 66% HP, Emberfang slams the ground and creates expanding fire rings. Getting hit scorches you for 2 seconds — usually fatal because he follows up with a bolt volley while you're still burning.

<Accordion title="Phase 2 details (open if you keep dying here)">
  <p><strong>Ring timing</strong>: rings expand every 1.2s in sets of three. Jump as each ring reaches your feet — the window is 0.4s per ring.</p>
  <p><strong>Why dash fails</strong>: dash has a 6s cooldown but the follow-up volley arrives 2s after the last ring. Jump the rings, save the dash for the volley.</p>
  <p><strong>Safe spots</strong>: the two stone braziers in the arena corners do not spawn rings. Standing behind one means you only ever jump two of the three rings.</p>
</Accordion>

**Counter**: Jump over each ring. The timing is generous (0.4s window per ring). Save your dash for the follow-up volley, not the rings.

## Phase 3: Inferno Enrage (33% – 0% HP)

Below 33% HP, Emberfang enters enrage: movement speed +30%, bolt volley becomes five bolts instead of three, and burning ground rings spawn twice as fast. The five-bolt spread still fans left-to-right, but the gaps close from "casual walk" to "deliberate strafe".

**Counter**: Pop your frost potion at the start of Phase 3. The frost buff cancels the scorch effect entirely, removing the deadliest mechanic from the fight. What remains is a five-bolt strafe and faster rings — both of which you've been practicing for two phases.

**Alternative — Mage (level 8):** Firebolt is fire damage and gets quartered here; take the **Ice Shard** skill instead (free at the Frostspire Approach trainer). Kiting Emberfang in a circle beats strafing for classes with lower HP — the fight becomes a solo dance with zero ring jumps.

**Alternative — Rogue (level 8):** Backstab the wind-up. Every bolt volley has a 0.8s wind-up during which his crit zone is exposed; a Backstab crit in that window deals 15% of his HP bar. Riskier, roughly two minutes faster.

## Common Mistakes

- **Dodging into the bolts.** New players panic-dash forward and eat all three bolts. Strafe, don't dash, in Phase 1.
- **Using dash on burning ground rings.** Dash has a 6-second cooldown. You need it for the bolt follow-up. Jump the rings instead.
- **Forgetting the frost potion.** Phase 3 without frost buff is a 50/50 coinflip. With it, the fight is essentially over.
- **Fighting him at level 6–7 "to save time".** This is the slowest possible path: his 180-damage bolts one-shot the level 7 HP pool after a single scorch tick. Grinding two boar packs to level 8 is faster than five failed attempts.
- **Selling the Molten Gems.** They drop at 35% and you need four for the Emberweave chest piece — see the [forging materials guide](/items/forging-materials-guide/) for the full shopping list.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="Can I beat Emberfang under-leveled?">
  Level 6 is possible with a Frostpike and five potions, but there is no reason to: the Oakwood Vale side quests take 15 minutes and give 400 coins plus enough XP for level 8. Under-leveled attempts mostly teach you the fight with worse rewards.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Emberfang respawn for farming?">
  Yes — he respawns 20 minutes after death, and the Molten Gem drop (35%) is the main early-game farm. Average yield is about 1 gem per 3 kills, or roughly 2,550 coins per hour counting the flat reward.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why did my fire weapon do almost no damage?">
  He is fire-resistant: fire deals 25% damage, neutral deals 100%, ice deals 200%. There is a fire-anvil shrine right before his arena that tricks new players into fire-imbuement — skip it.
</Accordion>
