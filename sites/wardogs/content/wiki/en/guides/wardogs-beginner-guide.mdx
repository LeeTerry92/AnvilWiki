## Your first Early Access session

WARDOGS is available on Steam. Install and launch WARDOGS, not the old Playtest application; finish pending game updates before selecting a server. Consult the server guide if you cannot find a match, and the troubleshooting guide if joining fails.

- Pick a server and read its rules before buying equipment.

- Start with a modest loadout and learn the current Control Zone before risking expensive equipment. This is an editorial suggestion, not a tested optimal build.

- Contribute through objectives, revives, transport or logistics; the publisher confirms these team actions reward cash.

- Read the current shop and unlock track. Season 1 changes invalidate some beta prices and progression shortcuts.

**Source status: Confirmed.**

## The WARDOGS match in one minute

WARDOGS puts up to 100 players into three teams: Valkyra, Lonestar and Manticore. The main objective is a randomized 2 x 2 km Control Zone inside a 256 km² battlefield.

Every 30 seconds, the team with the most players in the Control Zone earns one point. The first team to reach 100 points wins.

**Source status: Confirmed.**

## What to understand before your first match

- You begin with $10,000 and buy a custom loadout for each life.

- Cash persists between matches, so spending is part of the wider progression loop.

- There are no fixed classes; equipment and vehicles define the role you perform.

- Reviving teammates, transporting friendlies and controlling the objective can earn cash.

- Drop-in and drop-out support means players can join and leave ongoing battles.

## A practical first-match checklist

The official rules make the Control Zone the clearest place to orient yourself. Before spawning, identify what your team needs, choose equipment for that task and remember that support actions can contribute to both the team and your cash balance.

- Locate the current Control Zone and understand how your team is scoring.

- Choose one job for the life: fight, revive, transport, build or operate a vehicle.

- Treat purchases as role choices rather than a fixed class selection.

- Use local voice communication where appropriate; proximity voice is officially listed.

## Beta tutorial basics to learn first

Community tutorial footage shows that new players should understand the vendor loop before chasing advanced tactics. The useful beginner sequence is simple: buy a weapon and ammo, understand penetration and ricochet, add armor without ignoring weight, learn faster revives through medical gear and practice basic building with a hammer before relying on FOBs in a live match.

- Vendors matter because every life starts with a spending decision.

- Armor can improve survivability but adds weight, so movement cost belongs in loadout planning.

- Medical tools can make revives faster than bare-hands recovery.

- Specialist items such as hammers, medical bags and RPGs create the role you are playing.
