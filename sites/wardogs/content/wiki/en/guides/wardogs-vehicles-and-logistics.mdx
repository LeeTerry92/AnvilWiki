**Source snapshot:** Public WARDOGS Field Guide, reviewed September 14, 2026. Official announcements and historical community test-build data are distinct; prices, availability and planned features may change.

## Launch vehicle balance and unlock changes

Season 1 moves Artillery Tank to Career level 90 and Heavy Tank to Driver level 35. The changelog lists Career Artillery Tank at $500,000, Pilot Little Bird with miniguns at $50,000 and Pilot Z20 Lakota at $35,000. These are the named track entries, not an interchangeable per-life purchase-price table.

- Driver entries: URAL $35,000, Dune Buggy $25,000 and Kodiak Flatbed $35,000.

- Lakota received a balance pass and sluggish-pitch fix; helicopter input damping applies across input devices.

- Vehicle turrets have a maximum operator rotation speed and tank aiming received a DPI-related fix.

- 155mm, 122mm and flare deployables take more loadout space. Check the current loadout instead of copying a beta inventory.

**Source status: Confirmed.**

## Confirmed vehicle capabilities

Official descriptions confirm drivable ground vehicles and flyable aircraft. Helicopters can transport teammates and supplies, while armored vehicles include tanks and artillery-capable examples.

**Source status: Confirmed.**

## What logistics contributes

- Transporting friendly players can earn cash.

- Helicopters can move both players and supplies.

- Vehicles can support movement across the large battlefield and pressure around the objective.

- Construction and forward operating bases are part of the wider logistics fantasy described by the publisher.

## Vehicle choices are economic choices

Vehicle spending affects both immediate team capability and your continuing cash balance. Selected official launch costs and unlocks are listed above; a complete per-life cost comparison has not been independently tested here.

**Source status: Unconfirmed.**

## Alpha-observed vehicle roles

Community vehicle footage breaks vehicles into practical jobs: light helicopter transport, armed helicopter harassment, troop transport, artillery support, main battle tank pressure, anti-air defense and basic ground movement. These are useful content buckets for a guide, but exact unlock levels, prices and balance should be verified in the Early Access build.

**Source status: Unconfirmed.**

## Helicopter basics from community guides

Helicopter guide transcripts focus less on flashy flying and more on basic reliability: set keybinds, practice in the range, understand collective or throttle control, unlock seats for passengers, choose safer landing zones and buy enough countermeasures before flying into contested airspace.

- Passenger transport can generate cash through deployment and follow-up assists.

- Fuel, ammo, flares and repairs make aircraft an ongoing cost, not a free advantage.

- Transport helicopters can be profitable when they consistently move teammates without crashing.

- Armed variants may trade passenger capacity for damage output.

**Source status: Planned.**

## Launch content versus roadmap content

Fighter jets are named in the Steam Early Access plans as future content. They should not be treated as a confirmed launch-day vehicle until BULKHEAD publishes a more specific release statement.
