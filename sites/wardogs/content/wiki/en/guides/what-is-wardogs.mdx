## WARDOGS is available in Early Access

BULKHEAD's tactical all-out-warfare FPS launched on Steam on September 10, 2026. Team17 now lists it as available in Early Access and on GeForce NOW. Early Access is a playable development stage, not a completed version 1.0.

Start with the beginner guide for your first match, the server guide for joining and the patch notes for changes since beta. The September 10 launch trailer also announces a 2028 console window without an exact release day.

**Source status: Confirmed.**

## WARDOGS in one paragraph

WARDOGS is BULKHEAD's tactical take on large-scale multiplayer warfare. Up to 100 players split across three teams and fight for a randomized Control Zone using infantry weapons, vehicles, logistics, fortifications and environmental destruction.

## The persistent cash system

Players start with $10,000 and buy a custom loadout for each life. Team actions such as reviving allies, transporting players and controlling the objective reward cash. The balance persists between matches, making equipment and vehicle purchases longer-term decisions.

## Where WARDOGS sits as a shooter

Official video material repeatedly pushes back on calling WARDOGS a Battlefield killer. BULKHEAD describes it as its own thing: responsive enough to play clearly, but tactical enough that travel time, cash risk, no squad spawning, weapon handling, penetration, ricochet and paid loadouts create friction.

For readers comparing WARDOGS to Battlefield, Squad, Arma or extraction shooters, the clean answer is that WARDOGS borrows recognizable ideas but organizes them around three-team objective fighting and persistent money.

## What WARDOGS is not

- It is not presented as a Battle Royale.

- It is not presented as an Extraction Shooter.

- It does not use fixed classes; players build roles through purchased equipment and vehicles.

## Building, vehicles and destruction

Official descriptions include helicopters, armored vehicles, artillery tanks, forward operating bases, fortifications and destructible buildings. These systems connect infantry fighting with transport, support and logistics.

**Source status: Confirmed.**

## What Steam currently calls the Early Access state

Steam's September listing describes the Early Access version as a fully playable expression of the core vision: 100-player three-team battles, dynamic objectives, Cash and XP progression, gunplay, vehicles, logistics, support play and proximity voice chat. It also explicitly says balance, features and polish will continue to change.
