## Reading Bakurani's terrain

A river corridor with farmland and settlements below wooded slopes. The valley offers direct movement routes, while the surrounding ridges break up sightlines and overlook the approaches.

- A river valley links cultivated ground and built-up areas.

- Woodland and rising terrain surround the more accessible valley floor.

- The recorded names include villages, lumber sites, churches and numbered towers.

## Route-planning considerations

The following is practical planning advice inferred from the terrain, not a tested competitive route or a guarantee of live-build cover.

- Before deploying, compare the direct valley approach with a route that avoids exposed stretches. The shortest line is not necessarily the safest.

- Check the slope above a road before committing a transport. A visible ridge may offer observation, but climbing it also costs time away from the objective.

- Agree a pickup point with your squad. Use the map as a terrain reference, then confirm passable crossings and cover in the current match.
