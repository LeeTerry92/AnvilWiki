The September 10–14 official updates reviewed for this site do not validate this terrain's full live availability, every coordinate or every working name. ## Reading Ozeti's terrain

A built-up corridor surrounded by fields, orchard-like parcels and patches of trees. The terrain record includes a stadium, residential districts and church-related locations on the slopes.

- Road-connected buildings form a more concentrated settlement area.

- Field parcels and scattered wooded sections separate built-up locations.

- The working-name list includes a stadium, shops, residential areas and hillside churches.

## Route-planning considerations

The following is practical planning advice inferred from the terrain, not a tested competitive route or a guarantee of live-build cover.

- Choose an approach that gives your squad somewhere to regroup before entering a built-up area. An open field can leave little room to recover from a bad crossing.

- Check both the streets and higher ground when planning a route. Do not assume that a wall visible on the raster is intact, climbable or useful cover in the live build.

- Call the map Ozeti when coordinating with other players, and mention Europe only when comparing this guide with older community references.

## Availability and naming

The community record associates Ozeti with Closed Beta 2 from September 3, 2026. Older files use Europe or Madrid labels; these are not additional maps in this catalogue.

Location names and positions are taken from a community snapshot of test-build map records. They are useful callouts for this viewer, but are not developer-confirmed final names.

## What this map cannot tell you

- The current match's Control Zone, Hot Zone, safe zones or team positions.

- Whether a bridge, road, building entrance or interior is usable in the current build.

- Live vehicle, vendor, fuel, enemy or player-built structure locations.

- A guaranteed best route, spawn location or launch-day server rotation.
