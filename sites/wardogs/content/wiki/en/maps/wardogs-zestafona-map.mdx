## Reading Zestafona's terrain

An exposed landscape with sparse vegetation, field blocks and developed sites. The terrain and working-name records include rail facilities, warehouses, factories and water infrastructure.

- Open ridges and low vegetation offer less continuous canopy than the forested valley record.

- Agricultural parcels and developed sites provide more distinct navigation references.

- Industrial and rail-related working names make up a substantial part of the location list.

## Route-planning considerations

The following is practical planning advice inferred from the terrain, not a tested competitive route or a guarantee of live-build cover.

- Treat long, open approaches as a planning risk until you can assess them in game. Avoid assuming a broad empty area is safe simply because it has no marker.

- Use large industrial landmarks to coordinate a meeting point, but verify entrances and accessible interiors after spawning.

- Do not read these notes as a tested optimal route. This is terrain-based planning advice; objective placement, playable rotation and real combat cover still need live-build verification.

## What this map cannot tell you

- The current match's Control Zone, Hot Zone, safe zones or team positions.

- Whether a bridge, road, building entrance or interior is usable in the current build.

- Live vehicle, vendor, fuel, enemy or player-built structure locations.

- A guaranteed best route, spawn location or launch-day server rotation.
