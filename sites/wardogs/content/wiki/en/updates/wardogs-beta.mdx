**Source snapshot:** Public WARDOGS Field Guide, reviewed September 14, 2026. Official announcements and historical community test-build data are distinct; prices, availability and planned features may change.

## Playtest timeline

- **Sep 3 — Closed Beta 2**: Historical test opened at 19:00 UTC.

- **Sep 5–6 — Open phase and beta end**: Opened to everyone September 5; published end September 6 at 08:00 UTC.

- **Sep 10 — Steam Early Access released**: Paid access through the WARDOGS game, not the old Playtest client.

## Current access: use the Early Access game

WARDOGS is available on Steam following its September 10 Early Access launch. The September beta is over. Old invitations, pre-order access promises and the test client are not instructions for joining the paid game now.

## Why this historical page remains

The final beta opened to everyone on September 5 and its published window ended September 6 at 08:00 UTC. This page preserves that chronology for older links and returning players.

Future tests may have separate rules, but this archive does not announce a new test. Check official announcements for any new invitation; purchasing the current game and registering interest in future testing are different actions.
