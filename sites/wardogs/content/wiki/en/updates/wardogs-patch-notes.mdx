## Patch 0.11: announced maintenance, not a live-status claim

BULKHEAD announced Patch 0.11 on September 12 at 22:26 UTC, with maintenance scheduled for September 14 at 08:00 UTC (16:00 China Standard Time). The estimated interruption is about one hour. A scheduled end is not a confirmed recovery time.

This September 14 source review found the announcement, not a separate completion notice. Check the official announcement before assuming the maintenance is over or the patch is installed. This page does not monitor servers in real time.

**Source status: Planned.**

## What Patch 0.11 changes for finding a match

- Separate Community and Official server browsers; remove unnecessary filters from the Official browser.

- Improve Community filtering and add text search, plus Show Empty/Full quick controls.

- Keep community server IDs across map changes and restarts so returning players can use a persistent ID.

- Correct the Host destination and standardize alphabetical server ordering, including special characters and repeated spaces.

- Address additional GPU crashes and cash exploits. These are developer-listed fixes, not a guarantee that every crash is resolved.

**Source status: Time-sensitive.**

## Community bonus and economy rules

The announcement offers an additional 5% end-of-match cash bonus on community servers for a week alongside the update. This is not a permanent multiplier or a bonus on every cash action. An exact expiry timestamp is not specified, so verify availability in the current announcement and game.

BULKHEAD also reports expanded Asian server capacity. It warns that exploiting cash bugs can lead to timed bans and cash/XP resets, while promoting economy exploits or using XP-farming servers can lead to permanent bans. Earn through legitimate match play; do not treat a server name advertising boosted XP as official permission.

**Source status: Completed.**

## September 10: Launch Stability Hotfix #1 — released

The launch-day client update targeted inability to join servers, unexpected kicks and long login queues. The instructions at rollout were to close the game, install the Steam game update and relaunch. Players then returned to the queue while admission proceeded in controlled batches.

That sequence describes the September 10 incident. It does not mean everyone must still queue, or that the same fault is active now. Use the troubleshooting guide for a current problem rather than repeating historical emergency instructions indefinitely.

**Source status: Confirmed.**

## Season 1 launch baseline: why beta guides differ

The September 9, 22:46 UTC changelog was published before launch and is included here as the baseline. BULKHEAD says the weekend-test progression was replaced by a longer-term curve: levels 10–20 take more XP, while the curve flattens from level 35 onward.

Published cumulative XP thresholds include level 10 at 35,000, level 15 at 110,000, level 20 at 250,000 and level 35 at 500,000. These are not per-level XP costs or guaranteed playtime targets.

- Vendor FOB cost: $2,500 → $7,500. Vendor Large Hammer: $1,600 → $2,400.

- Artillery Tank moves to Career level 90; Heavy Tank moves to Driver level 35.

- Voice reliability, helicopter input damping, tank aiming and multiple crash paths received launch changes.

- DLSS Frame Generation can be disabled without restarting according to the changelog. This is a control change, not a measured FPS recommendation.

**Source status: In development.**
