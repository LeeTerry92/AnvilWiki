## How to read Playtest footage

These clips are edited from beta-era community footage. They are useful for seeing the objective loop, cash decisions, flexible roles and field mechanics, but they are not proof of September 10 balance, prices, performance or final controls.

Each video keeps its original source credit and uses a local MP4 without a subtitle track. The surrounding text carries the context so the footage remains uncluttered.

## What the footage can establish

- Control Zone rather than eliminations is the central match objective.

- Cash decisions connect loadouts, equipment and vehicles across deployments.

- Roles are assembled through purchased gear instead of fixed classes.

- Infantry, vehicles, logistics and construction share the same battlefield loop.

**Source status: Unconfirmed.**

- Live payout values and the Cash-to-Gold conversion rate.

- Whether beta bugs, routes and strong positions remain in the launch build.
