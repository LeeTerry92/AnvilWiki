## What changed in the launch trailer

BULKHEAD's September 10 launch trailer and its accompanying Steam announcement give a 2028 console window. This supersedes the older no-window wording on this site.

The announcement says consoles, without settling every hardware model, edition, exact launch day or crossplay arrangement. Do not turn a year into a guaranteed date for a specific PlayStation or Xbox model.

## What can you play now?

Steam lists Windows support and the September 10 Early Access release. Team17 also lists GeForce NOW availability. Cloud streaming is not the same thing as a native console release.

## Crossplay and controller support

A console announcement does not establish crossplay. Earlier developer material discussed partial PC gamepad support, but the reviewed launch notice does not provide a new device-by-device support matrix or aim-assist values.
