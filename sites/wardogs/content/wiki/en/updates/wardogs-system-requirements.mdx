**Source snapshot:** Public WARDOGS Field Guide, reviewed September 14, 2026. Official announcements and historical community test-build data are distinct; prices, availability and planned features may change.

**Source status: Confirmed.**

## Minimum requirements

- 64-bit processor and operating system; Windows 10.

- Intel Core i5-8600 or AMD Ryzen 5 3500.

- 16 GB RAM.

- Nvidia GTX 1660 or AMD Radeon RX 590.

- 50 GB available storage and broadband internet.

- Official target: 1080p Low at 60 fps, upscaled.

**Source status: Confirmed.**

## Recommended requirements

- 64-bit processor and operating system; Windows 11.

- Intel Core i7-12700K or AMD Ryzen 7 5700X.

- 16 GB RAM.

- Nvidia RTX 3070 or AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT.

- 50 GB available storage and broadband internet.

- Official targets: 1440p Medium at 70+ fps native, or 4K Medium at 60+ fps upscaled.

**Source status: Time-sensitive.**

## Requirements can change

WARDOGS is available in Early Access, so hardware targets and storage requirements can change. The Steam store page should remain the primary reference; its FPS targets are not independent benchmark results.

**Source status: Confirmed.**

## Steam platform, anti-cheat and language notes

- The September 14 Steam listing identifies Elytra as kernel-level anti-cheat. Older Easy Anti-Cheat references on this site have been superseded.

- Windows 10 and Windows 11 are the only operating systems named in the published requirement tables.

- Broadband internet is required for the online PvP game.

- Steam currently lists 14 interface languages; English is the only language marked for full audio.

## Performance context from official Q&amp;A

In the official Q&amp;A video, BULKHEAD says performance has been part of development from early on and describes testing against a practical baseline PC rather than assuming only high-end hardware. That does not replace the Steam specification table, but it explains why performance and input responsiveness matter to the developer.

Because WARDOGS is a large multiplayer Unreal Engine 5 game with many players concentrated around a combat zone, keep this page focused on the current Steam targets and update it when the store listing changes.
