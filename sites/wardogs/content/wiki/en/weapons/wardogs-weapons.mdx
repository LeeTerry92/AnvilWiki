## Early Access verification: what this catalogue can prove

The 33 records below remain a community test-build reference, not a verified complete launch arsenal. Weapon images and catalogue interactions are retained. Unverified launch damage, recoil and prices are not invented.

The official Season 1 baseline confirms selected changes: Career Deagle moves to level 85; 762x54mm AP to 82; 556mm AP to 83; Medic PP-19 50-round drum to 29. Recon 6–10x MRAD and MOA scope track entries change to $40,000 and $45,000 respectively. These do not establish all current weapon prices.

Where a beta record differs from those dated official changes, use the official change for that field and verify the remaining values in the current game. No best-weapon or TTK ranking is claimed.

## Choose a loadout you can replace

Compare the weapon price with your available cash before choosing a role. A cheaper rifle leaves room for ammunition and other equipment; an expensive unlock does not remove the cost of buying the weapon for a deployment.

Use the homepage profiles to compare eight representative weapons, then filter this catalogue by category or calibre. These are reference choices, not a ranked tier list.

## Read the numbers in context

A listed effective range is not a guarantee of a kill at that distance. Fire rate alone does not establish damage, recoil, accuracy or time to kill. This guide does not manufacture damage bars or use real-world firearm specifications to fill missing game statistics.

The reviewed community records span pre-release tests. Check the current in-game shop for prices, unlock requirements and availability before spending your cash.
